Privacy statement

Ledgerfern follows the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

  • When you buy: your name, email address and order details, collected by our checkout provider Payhip. Card or PayPal details are handled by Stripe or PayPal. We never see or store them.
  • When you download the free checklist: your email address.
  • When you visit this site: anonymous page-view statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't track individuals).

What we don't collect

The workbooks run on your own device or in your own Google account. We can't see anything you type into them.

How we use it

We use it to deliver your purchase, help with support, and (only if you opted in) send occasional emails about tax deadlines and new editions. Every email has an unsubscribe link. We never sell or share your information.

Your rights

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Contact the support email on your purchase receipt.
