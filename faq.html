Frequently asked questions

Which apps does it work in?

Microsoft Excel (2016 or later, Windows or Mac), Google Sheets (upload the .xlsx to Google Drive, then open it with Google Sheets) and Apple Numbers. It doesn't use macros.

Do I need to be GST registered?

No. Set "Are you GST registered?" to No and the workbook skips the GST maths, so your income and expenses count at their full amounts.

Is this tax advice?

No. Ledgerfern workbooks are record-keeping and estimating tools. They show every rate and date they use, so you can check them against ird.govt.nz. If your situation is complex, talk to a tax agent.

Will it file my GST return or IR3?

No. You copy the figures into myIR yourself. That only takes a few minutes, because the boxes are numbered to match the IRD forms.

What happens next tax year?

Tax rates and dates can change, so a new edition comes out before each 1 April. Previous buyers get a discount on the new edition. Your existing file keeps working, and you can edit the rates on its Settings tab.

How do I get my files?

Straight after payment, on screen and by email, through Payhip. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then contact us.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Ask within 14 days for any reason. See our refund policy. Your rights under the Consumer Guarantees Act are not affected.

Can I share it with friends?

Your licence is for your own business (or your household's rental properties). Please don't resell it or share the files publicly.
