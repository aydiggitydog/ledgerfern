Rental Property Workbook

For NZ landlords with 1–5 rentals who want clear numbers at tax time. It applies the current rules: 100% mortgage interest deductibility and ring-fenced rental losses.

Buy for $39Bundle with the Sole Trader workbook: $59

What's inside

  • One Transactions list for every property. Pick the property and the category from dropdowns.
  • A Summary for each property, plus a total, under IR3R headings: gross rents, rates, insurance, interest, repairs, agent's fees, legal fees, depreciation, other.
  • Mortgage interest at 100% (with the % editable on Settings).
  • Ring-fencing: losses brought forward are used against profits first, and new losses are carried forward.
  • Capital improvements and loan principal are tracked but kept out of your deductions.
  • Monthly cash flow across the whole portfolio.
  • A filled-in example file.

Honest limits

Uses the portfolio basis for ring-fencing. Doesn't cover the bright-line test, mixed-use holiday homes or depreciation calculations (enter the figure from your depreciation schedule). Residential rent is GST-exempt, so GST isn't tracked. Not tax advice.
